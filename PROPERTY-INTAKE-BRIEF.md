# Your Property Website — What We Need From You

*A short guide to everything we need to start building your property's website.*

---

## 1. Welcome — how this works

Building your website is mostly our job. This document covers the part only you
can provide: the facts about your property, how guests should reach you, and your
photos. We take care of everything else — the design, the writing, the technical
setup and getting it online.

A few things to keep in mind as you go:

- **Answer what you can now.** Skip anything you're unsure about — we'll fill the
  gaps together on a short call.
- **Bullet points are fine.** You don't need to write polished text. Give us your
  notes and we'll turn them into the finished wording, then send it back for your
  approval.
- **Nothing here is final.** Prices, wording, photos and even the web address can
  all be changed later.

Section 2 is the short list of essentials that lets us start. Everything after
that can follow over the first week or so.

---

## 2. The essentials — enough for us to start

We can begin as soon as we have:

- Property name
- Type of property (villa, house, apartment, etc.)
- Town and country
- Rough size — bedrooms, bathrooms, and how many guests it sleeps
- A few sentences about the place (informal is completely fine)
- The full address
- A phone number for guests to call, plus a Facebook page or website if you have
  one
- The email address where booking enquiries should be sent (this stays private)
- At least 8 photos, or a link to where they are — a starter set is fine, you can
  add more later

That's all we need to get going. The rest of this document fills in the detail
and can come a bit at a time.

## 8. Prices and booking  *(optional)*

Your site can include a live availability calendar and an enquiry form, or it can
simply present the property with a "contact us" button. **You can skip this whole
section for now** and add it later.

If you'd like the calendar and enquiry form from the start:

**8.1 Price per night, and the currency**
*Example: 10,000 THB per night.* You can also launch with no price shown, if you
prefer to quote guests directly.

**8.2 Any extra fees?** (cleaning, service, etc.)

**8.3 Minimum and maximum nights per stay** (if any)
*Example: minimum 2 nights, no maximum*

**8.4 How far ahead can guests book?**
*Example: up to 12 months ahead*

**8.5 Dates already booked or blocked**
The calendar isn't linked automatically to Airbnb or Booking.com — it's updated
manually. Let us know any dates that are already unavailable so the site can go
live with the right information, and we'll agree together how future updates will
be handled.

## 9. Photos

Good photos make the biggest difference to how the site looks. We handle all the
editing, sizing and layout — we just need good originals from you.

**Please:**

- Send the **original files**, straight from the camera or phone
- **Don't crop, resize or compress** them first, and avoid heavy filters
- Send **at least 8**; **15–30 is ideal** — we'll choose the best set
- Share them as a **folder link** (Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer) rather than
  pasted into a chat or document

**Useful shots to include:**

- The property from outside — your best, widest view
- The pool and outdoor areas
- The main living room
- The kitchen and dining area
- Each bedroom, or the best few
- The nicest bathroom
- Garden, terrace, balcony or BBQ area
- The view, the beach nearby, or a nice sunset — something that captures the feeling

**9.2 Please confirm:** the photos are yours, or you have permission to use them.
*(We can't use images taken from Booking.com, Airbnb or other listing sites.)*

## 10. Domain and branding  *(optional)*

None of this needs to be ready before we start. We can launch on a temporary web
address and switch to your real one later.

**10.1 Web address (domain)** — do you already have one? If so, what is it? If
not, let us know and we'll talk you through the options.

**10.2 Logo** — if you have one, send it in whatever format you have. If not,

**10.3 Colours and style** — if you have brand colours or a particular look in
mind, tell us or send examples. Otherwise we'll use a clean, warm style that
suits holiday properties.

## 11. Language

**Your website is built in English by default.** This is our standard.

- The menus, buttons and standard labels are also shown in **Thai
  automatically** — we take care of that, at no extra cost.
- Your **description text** — the wording about your property — is written in
  **English**.
- If you'd like a full **professional Thai translation** of that description
  text, we offer it as an **optional paid add-on**. It can be added at launch or
  any time afterwards, and it's never required to go live.
